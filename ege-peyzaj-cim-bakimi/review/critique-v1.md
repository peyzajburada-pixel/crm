# Critique: draft-v1 (20 s, 9:16)

Viewed from: sheet.jpg (1 fps), strip-5.4s, strip-12.4s, strip-16.4s, report.txt.

## 1. 🔴 The ending does not land (17.0-20.0)
- Frame: sheet 00:18 / 00:19; strip-16.4s tiles 5-12.
- The tagline "Baharda yeşili siz görün." is not natural Turkish. A native speaker hears "you see the green", which says nothing. It is also tiny, about 2% of the frame height, so it cannot be read on a phone.
- There is no way to reach the company: no phone number, website or handle. A viewer knows the brand name but not what to do next.
- The card is a still for about 2 s (18-20 s, flagged "nothing moves"). It is also 6.2 LU louder than the rest of the film (-11.0 LUFS against a -17.2 median), so the sound jumps at the moment it should resolve.
- Smallest fix: change the line to "Baharda bahçeniz yemyeşil olsun." at about twice the size. Add one contact line, such as a phone number or website, under it. Bring the end-card music down about 5 dB to match the scenes before it.

## 2. 🔴 Station 4 is unfinished and contradicts its own tip (13.0-16.0)
- Frame: sheet 00:13 (top half blank cream), strip-12.4s tile 5 (sky not drawn on the cut), sheet 00:15.
- The first frame after the 13.0 cut has an empty top half and no "4" badge, so it looks broken.
- The caption "Yağmurda sulamayı azalt" only appears at about 15 s, leaving about 1 s to read it before the 16.0 cut. Every other station shows its caption at once.
- The hand turns the dial all the way to KAPALI (off), while the caption says "reduce". The sprinkler also keeps spraying under the rain.
- Smallest fix: draw the sky and the badge from the first frame. Show the caption on the cut (13.2 s). Stop the needle halfway between AÇIK and KAPALI and let the spray shrink.

## 3. 🔴 Station captions are too small, and the rake passes through one (2-16 s)
- Frame: sheet 00:03 / 00:07 / 00:12; strip-12.4s tiles 1-4.
- The caption pills ("Biçme boyu 5 cm", "Havalandır, tohumla", "Yaprakları topla") have text only about 10-11 px tall on the 960 px frame. On a phone they are too small to read, and these captions carry the whole message while there is no narration.
- At 11-13 s the rake handle runs straight through the "Yaprakları topla" pill.
- Smallest fix: make the caption text about 1.8 times larger (about 20 px or more at 540x960) and move each pill clear of moving props. For the rake, move the pill to the left of the handle or bring it to the front.

## Smaller notes
- Sound: the 2-5 kHz band is harsh, +6.6 dB at 10.5-13 and +5.2 dB at 13-16. Soften the lead in those scenes. Integrated loudness is -14.7 LUFS, which is within the -14 ±1 target.
- 6.0 s: the wipe between stations 1 and 2 is fine in motion. Station 3 has no gardener, so the "work trail" idea weakens there.
- The Turkish register is mixed: station captions use the informal "sen" imperative, and the tagline uses the formal "siz". Pick one.
